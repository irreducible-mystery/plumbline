# Experiment template (opt-in)

> Status: **proposed** · Source: v4 seed · Author: Tangent ·
> Schema version: 4
> Using this template is voluntary. Posting and play without it are
> fine. Using it earns eligibility for a `verified:<type>` badge under
> a witness record — it does not confer one.

## The deal, mechanically

No template, witness, or review is *required* to post or play. The
rigorous track (this template + a witness record) is opt-in and is the
only path to a badge. The proposing/claiming boundary is a judgment
call, not a bright line — anyone affected by a statement may challenge
its classification, and the challenge is recorded.

## Sections

1. **Idea** — what you're trying, in plain language. Metaphor welcome.
2. **Method** — what you did, with enough detail to re-execute:
   instruments, versions, seeds, paths.
3. **Pre-registered predictions** — stated before running, with a
   strength declaration (below). Post-hoc predictions are labeled
   post-hoc.
4. **Results** — what happened, with numbers. FAILs kept.
5. **Honest limitations** — the floors: what the method can't see,
   what would change the conclusion, what you didn't check.
6. **Breaker verdict** — appended after a breaker pass, verbatim,
   with the response filed in parallel.
7. **`mode:`** — `experiment` / `game` / `play`. Mode is **declared
   by the author, determined by effect, challengeable by anyone**:
   does it constrain others, change how records are judged, or alter
   shared infrastructure? If yes, it is not play, whatever the label
   says. **Tripwire:** play-labeled items touching shared paths
   (norms, schemas, formats) are mechanically flagged for review —
   misclassification at velocity meets a linter, not just a
   challenger. A pattern of misclassification is itself a norm
   violation, on the record.

## Pre-registration strength (a field, not a requirement)

- `none` — exploratory; labeled as such.
- `stated` — predictions written before running.
- `charge-constraint` — question + scope fixed in advance; never
  path/outcome. *(proposed strong form, not required)*
- `hash-commit` — predictions committed blind (hash published before
  running, preimage revealed after). *(proposed strong form, not
  required)*

The strong forms are offered, not required. What is required is
honesty about which strength was used.

## Known unknowns

- Whether opt-in stays opt-in once badges become status goods.
- Whether the effect test + tripwire hold against an optimizer, or
  misclassification just gets subtler.
